# Nîşeyên Wergerê — Translation Notes

*Ev pel ayetên ku destê mirovî an biryarek wergerê guhert nîşan
dide. Nîşe bi Îngilîzî ne — ew rêziman û çavkaniyên Îbranî
vedibêjin.*

This file records every verse where a human hand or a ruling
touched the Kurmancî rendering. Rails: the Selah Kurmanji
discipline (Yahwe / Elohîm at the Name seat; ⟨את⟩ total; ⟨…⟩
marks supplied words only).

## Hand-rendered / hand-spliced verses (2026-08-30)

- **Hosea 2:6** — the chair's ONE fully hand-written verse: the
  no-mercy verse ("Û ⟨את⟩ li zarokên wê ez rehmê nakim…"),
  rendered by hand from the Hebrew tokens after every automated
  pass refused it — the verse its own book reverses at 2:25.
- **1 Kings 18:21** — the Baal-copula: ⟨is⟩ → ⟨be⟩ (subjunctive)
  in both occurrences ("ger Baal ⟨be⟩" — Elijah's if-clause).
- **Deuteronomy 6:13** — serve-⟨אתו⟩ → wî.
- **Job 16:21** — the mediator verse, with Eloah at its seat.
- **Haggai 2:10** — bi destê (by the hand of).
- **Ezekiel 1:3** — Êl at its seat ("Yê Êl xurt dike" in the
  name's gloss).
- **2 Kings 6:5** — a stray marker in the token line removed.
- Suffix pronouns ruled: ⟨אתו⟩ → wî · ⟨אתם⟩ → wan · ⟨אתכם⟩ → we;
  H859 (you) → tu; ית (Aramaic object marker) → wan.

## Per-token review verdicts — the Xwedê / Xudan floor

Xwedê (the Kurmanji Bible's god-word) and Xudan (lord-title) were
reviewed verse by verse. Xudan over human lords: LAWFUL. At the
true-God seat: healed to Elohîm (four Xwedê seats incl.
Ecclesiastes 12:1's Creator), with v1-style possessives kept
('Xwedayê te' → 'Elohîmê te'). The xwedî = OWNER cognate ruled
lawful over בעל/אדני ownership rows.
